PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 22, 2026 • VERSION 3.0

This Privacy Policy explains how PLACEHOLDER_REGISTERED_BUSINESS_NAME, operating in Nigeria ("BrainSweep", "we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you play the BrainSweep web game or interact with the BrainSweep embeddable widget on a partner website ("Publisher").

PLAIN-ENGLISH SUMMARY: BrainSweep stores your game settings, scores, streaks, and achievements locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not set tracking cookies by default. If you choose to save a score to the public leaderboard, we collect your chosen username, email address, and optional marketing preference. If you click "Accept" on the analytics prompt, we count anonymous gameplay events to improve the game. We never sell personal data.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT & STORE

  • On-Device Local Storage (localStorage): Your theme preference (brainsweep_theme), game settings (brainsweep_settings_v1), personal statistics (stats_easy, stats_medium, stats_hard), unlocked achievements (brainsweep_achievements_v1), Daily Sweep streak record, offline best score, and your analytics consent choice (brainsweep_cookie_consent_v1: accepted or declined). This data stays in your browser until you clear your site data.
  • Optional Leaderboard Registration: If you choose to submit your score to the global leaderboard, we collect your chosen username (3–16 alphanumeric characters), your email address (used only to protect your username and recover your scores), your game score, completion time in milliseconds, round identifier, and whether you ticked the optional "Send me game updates" box.
  • Consent-Gated Anonymous Analytics: Only if analytics is enabled in our configuration and you click Accept on the consent banner (or if a host Publisher passes consent=1 to an embedded widget), we record anonymous events: game_start, game_end (score, difficulty, pattern, duration, mistakes, completion flag), first_play, return_visit, daily_sweep_complete, share_click, leaderboard_register, embed_load (referrer domain), and embed_cta_click. Your email address and username are never sent to analytics.

2. HOW WE USE & VALIDATE DATA

  • To run the game, remember your preferences, and calculate your Daily Sweep streaks and achievements on your device.
  • To display top scores (username, score, and completion time only — never email addresses) on the public Today, This Week, and All-Time leaderboards.
  • To apply basic server-side score limits (checking maximum targets per difficulty, minimum plausible completion time, duplicate round IDs, and a 45-second submission rate limit). Please note honestly that client-side browser games cannot be made 100% cheat-proof.
  • If you explicitly ticked the optional marketing updates checkbox during registration, to send occasional game updates (you may unsubscribe at any time).

3. SUB-PROCESSORS & INFRASTRUCTURE

We use the following third-party service providers to host and operate BrainSweep:

  • Vercel Inc.: Hosts the static web application and delivers game files over HTTPS.
  • Google LLC (Google Apps Script & Google Sheets): Processes and stores optional leaderboard submissions (username, email, scores, completion times, and marketing opt-in timestamp).
  • Analytics Provider (Optional): When enabled in configuration (e.g., Plausible, Umami, PostHog, or GA4) and consented to by the user, processes anonymous gameplay event counts.

  • How to Exercise Your Rights: Email PLACEHOLDER_WORKING_EMAIL with your leaderboard username and the email address used during registration. We will process deletion or access requests within 30 days.
  • Retention Period: On-device localStorage remains until you clear your browser data. Leaderboard records are retained while the leaderboard is active or until you request deletion.
  • Regulatory Complaints: If you believe your data protection rights under Nigerian law have been infringed, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) (ndpc.gov.ng).
