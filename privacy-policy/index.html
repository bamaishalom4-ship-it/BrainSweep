PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 20, 2026 • VERSION 2.0 (GDPR & CCPA COMPLIANT)

This Privacy Policy explains how BrainSweep ("MadeByKubo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, and safeguards data when end-users ("Players") interact with the BrainSweep cognitive web game and its B2B embeddable iframe widget deployed across partner publisher websites ("Publishers").

SUMMARY FOR PLAYERS & PUBLISHERS: BrainSweep is built on a privacy-by-design architecture. We use strictly scoped cookies and browser localStorage for user preferences and session analytics, process anonymized gameplay telemetry (reaction splits and cumulative scores), and fully comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We never sell personal information to third parties.

1. CATEGORIES OF DATA WE COLLECT

When you play BrainSweep directly or via an embedded widget on a Publisher's website, we process the following minimized data categories:

  • Anonymized Gameplay Telemetry: Session scores, cumulative difficulty scores (Easy, Medium, Hard), extraction accuracy percentages, reaction-time split intervals, and selected trajectory/difficulty configurations.
  • Local Preferences & Consent State: Browser localStorage keys storing your chosen color theme (brainsweep_theme), audio mute settings, gameplay modifiers, and cookie consent status (brainsweep_cookie_consent_v1).
  • Optional Leaderboard Profile (Direct Mode Only): If a Player voluntarily registers for the Global Leaderboard, we collect a user-selected callsign (username) and email address solely to deduplicate and attribute cumulative scores. In B2B Embed Mode (?embed=true), registration prompts are disabled by default.
  • Technical & Analytics Metadata: Anonymized aggregate performance metrics (device viewport dimensions, frame-rate stability, and parent referrer domain for Publisher license verification).

2. COOKIES, LOCAL STORAGE & ANALYTICS

BrainSweep uses lightweight first-party cookies and HTML5 localStorage to deliver a seamless, zero-latency gaming experience inside embedded iframes:

  • Essential Functional Storage: Retains your active difficulty, trajectory vector, color theme, offline high scores, and cookie banner acknowledgment so you do not have to reconfigure the widget on every visit.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used to measure aggregate widget engagement (session starts, completion rates, and average play duration) for Publisher reporting. All analytics identifiers are pseudonymized and rotated.

Players can clear local storage or block cookies at any time via their browser settings. Upon first loading the widget, a non-intrusive consent banner allows Players to acknowledge storage usage before preferences are persisted.

3. GDPR COMPLIANCE (EUROPEAN ECONOMIC AREA & UK)

For Players located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we process data under the following legal bases pursuant to GDPR Article 6:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For optional analytics cookies and voluntary leaderboard profile registration.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): For delivering the core interactive game mechanics, preventing automated score tampering, and providing aggregated, non-identifiable engagement telemetry to host Publishers.

Your GDPR Rights: You have the right to request access, rectification, erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"), restriction of processing, and data portability. Because gameplay telemetry is anonymized, local data can also be erased immediately by clearing site data in your browser.

4. CCPA / CPRA COMPLIANCE (CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information: BrainSweep does not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to Know & Delete: California residents may request disclosure or deletion of any linked leaderboard profile record by contacting us at the address below.
  • Non-Discrimination: Exercising your privacy rights will never degrade gameplay access or widget performance.

5. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

All data transmitted between the BrainSweep widget and our cloud endpoints is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 (HTTPS). Anonymized gameplay metrics are retained for aggregate benchmarking, while voluntary leaderboard records are retained until the Player requests deletion.

6. CONTACT & DATA PROTECTION INQUIRIES

For privacy inquiries, GDPR/CCPA data subject requests, or B2B compliance questions, please contact the BrainSweep Data Protection Officer at: privacy@madebykubo.com.
