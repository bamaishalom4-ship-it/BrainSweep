How BrainSweep Works

BrainSweep is a high-speed visual-motor cognitive trainer designed to sharpen peripheral awareness, saccadic eye movements, and selective attention.

1. Objective & The "Gap" Rule

Find and drag target numbers out of the arena in ascending order within 60 seconds. Distractor numbers are randomly interleaved across the full number range—check NEXT TARGET and ignore the gaps!

2. Stages, Ratios & Distractors

  • EASY: Pool 20 (1–20) → 12 Targets, 8 Distractors (60% ratio, ~5s/target)
  • MEDIUM: Pool 36 (1–36) → 20 Targets, 16 Distractors (~55% ratio, ~3s/target)
  • HARD: Pool 50 (1–50) → 25 Targets, 25 Distractors (50% ratio, ~2.4s/target, High Speed)

3. Cognitive Skills Trained

Selective Attention & Inhibitory Control: Suppressing impulse grabs on interleaved distractors.
Working Memory Mode: Briefly flashes each new target before masking the HUD as ?.
