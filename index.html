HOW TO PLAY

RULES

Spot the target numbers inside the moving arena and drag or flick them across the dashed drop zone in strict ascending order within 60 seconds.

Decoy ("gap") numbers are randomly mixed into the pool. Check the centered NEXT TARGET indicator (or the Ghost Trail number spawned where you dropped your last target) and skip the decoys — grabbing a decoy costs −1.0s to −2.0s!

SCORE & DAILY SWEEP

Every target number you extract before the 60-second timer expires counts toward your Session Score:

  • • EASY: Up to 12 points per round (−1.0s decoy penalty)
  • • MEDIUM: Up to 20 points per round (−1.5s decoy penalty)
  • • HARD: Up to 25 points per round (−2.0s decoy penalty)

Play the seeded Daily Sweep every day to build your 🔥 streak and compete on the Today, This Week, and All-Time Best leaderboards!
