HOW TO PLAY

RULES

Scan the moving numbers inside the arena and drag or flick valid target numbers across the dashed extraction border in strict ascending order within 60 seconds.

Distractor ("gap") numbers are randomly interleaved within the number pool. Check the centered NEXT TARGET indicator (or the Ghost Trail number spawned where you dropped your last target) and ignore the distractor gaps!

SCORE

Every valid target number you extract before the 60-second timer expires counts toward your Session Score:

  • • EASY: Up to 12 points per session (Pool of 20)
  • • MEDIUM: Up to 20 points per session (Pool of 36)
  • • HARD: Up to 25 points per session (Pool of 50, High Speed)

At the end of each round, your Session Score is automatically added to your cumulative Total Score on the Global Leaderboard for that difficulty!
